Who we are

Meet YTDEVENTS

YTDEVENTS is an event planning and hospitality brand focused on helping people and organisations create meaningful gatherings. From event management and decoration to catering, small chops, cocktails, cakes, training and consultancy, we bring connected services together under one roof.

Our approach starts with listening. We learn what matters to you, shape a plan around your priorities and work through the details with care.

  • Creative ideas tailored to your occasion
  • Service options for private and corporate events
  • Attention to atmosphere, presentation and guest experience
  • A collaborative process from enquiry to event day
